## Content pillars

Six recurring arguments, two at each of the three times in the [Constitution](../theory/01-foundation/00-tcg-constitution.md). Every public piece advances at least one. The main point sits in the first: the kind of cost in the way should decide how you sell, and most teams sell one way regardless.

| **Time0, the market** | Sell to the cost in the way | Buying costs a buyer three things beyond price: finding a seller it trusts, getting its organization to agree, and making the product work. The kind in the way decides how to sell. A leader can read it from the workflow and the buyer's organization, and most teams copy a playbook. ## Three rules for a public piece

- **No math.** The accounting keeps the argument honest and is not shown.
- **Define plug-in and built-in at first use.** "Plug-in" also names a software add-on. The published toaster and bridge pair is retired.
- **Health tech supplies the examples,** built from public patterns. The author's own deals supply hypotheses and are not cited as evidence.
